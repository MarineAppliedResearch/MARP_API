# MARP Governance

This document describes how MARP is governed as an open-source project.

## Purpose

This governance model exists to:

- provide technical continuity
- make decision-making understandable
- encourage outside participation
- preserve a stable official MARP distribution
- allow maintainership to expand as the community grows
- keep the project aligned with its ecological and organizational purpose

## Founding steward

Marine Applied Research and Exploration (MARÉ) is the founding steward of MARP, the current administrator of the official repository, and the organization responsible for the original production platform from which MARP grew. MARÉ remains an ongoing participant in project direction and stewardship.

As founding steward, MARÉ retains special responsibility and authority regarding:

- the official repository and project infrastructure
- official MARP releases
- organizational and legal obligations
- official branding
- alignment of the roadmap with MARP's ecological mission
- deployments and systems operated by MARÉ

This is stewardship and operational responsibility, not exclusive ownership of the open-source community. Community members, independent contributors, and other organizations are welcome participants in the project's technical direction.

## Project founder and Lead Maintainer

Isaac Assegai Travers is the project founder and initial Lead Maintainer of MARP.

The Lead Maintainer:

- coordinates the technical direction of the open-source project
- reviews major architectural decisions
- coordinates releases with MARÉ
- helps maintain consistency across the API, frontend, data, processing, and machine-learning systems
- facilitates community discussion
- makes final technical decisions when consensus cannot be reached


## Decision-making model

- Technical decisions should first be discussed with relevant maintainers and contributors.
- Consensus is preferred where practical.
- The Lead Maintainer makes the final technical decision when consensus cannot be reached.
- Decisions involving MARÉ's legal obligations, official infrastructure, branding, production deployments, or organizational commitments require coordination with MARÉ.
- Official release and roadmap decisions should reflect both technical direction and MARÉ's stewardship responsibilities.

## Maintainers

Additional maintainers may be invited based on:

- sustained constructive contribution
- technical judgment
- reliability
- respectful collaboration
- understanding of MARP architecture and scientific purpose

New maintainers should be approved by the existing maintainers and the Lead Maintainer. Maintainership is based on contribution and trust, not organizational affiliation alone.

## Maintainer removal

Removing a maintainer should normally require:

- a documented reason
- an opportunity for discussion
- a two-thirds vote of active maintainers

The Lead Maintainer may override this outcome when necessary to protect project continuity, repository integrity, legal or organizational obligations, security, community safety, or the scientific and technical integrity of MARP. This override exists as a safeguard for exceptional circumstances, not as a routine or punitive mechanism.

## MARÉ's continuing authority

MARÉ retains authority over the official repository, official releases, official branding, production systems operated by MARÉ, and organizational commitments related to MARP.

The Apache License 2.0 permits community members to create and maintain independent forks of MARP. This is a legally permitted and expected part of an open-source project. However:

- an independent fork is not automatically an official MARP release
- use of official branding is governed separately from the software license
- an independent fork must not imply endorsement by MARÉ

## Governance scope

This governance document applies to:

- open-source source-code development
- technical architecture
- contribution review
- maintainership
- official open-source releases
- the technical project roadmap

This document does not govern:

- ecological datasets
- survey data
- video or imagery
- partner-owned data
- model weights
- restricted research materials
- private deployments
- systems operated by organizations using MARP

## Governance amendments

Changes to this governance document should be:

1. proposed in writing
2. discussed with maintainers
3. reviewed by the Lead Maintainer
4. coordinated with MARÉ, given its role as founding steward and official repository administrator
