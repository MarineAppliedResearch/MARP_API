What MARP is

MARP is where a survey turns into science.

A dive comes home with hours of video of the seafloor, and everything living in it is still waiting to be found.

MARP is what carries that from the camera to the answer. Everything a survey brings back stays connected to everything learned from it, so no stage begins by rebuilding what the last one already knew.

The first pass over the footage can run automatically, so possible observations are already waiting when a biologist sits down.

Stage by stage.

  1. Collect the video
  2. Line it up with the navigation
  3. Find possible observations
  4. Expert review
  5. Analyze and report
